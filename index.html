    How the cost is calculated

    Monthly cost = Σ (unit price × usage volume). Users × daily-active share × activity per user × 30.4 days gives the month's chat turns, voice turns and care logs. Every usage cost scales from those volumes. Fixed infrastructure is priced for 730 hours a month.

    Pilot calibration plan

    Calibrate on usage quantities, not dollars: replace each assumption with its observed value and let the model recompute cost from verified prices. That keeps "we misjudged usage" apart from "we misjudged prices."

    1. Freeze the forecast before the pilot starts: save the base inputs and per-driver forecasts with the date and cohort size.
    2. Week 1 smoke check: confirm every collection path returns numbers, and that no text or identifiers leave the environment.
    3. Compare weekly, driver by driver. Flag any driver outside its Low–High bound, or more than 25% off base.
    4. Update at weeks 2 and 4, and at pilot end. Set base to the observed median. With at least 5 users and 2 weeks of data, set Low/High to the observed 10th/90th percentiles.
    5. Cross-check observed AWS usage quantities (compute hours, DetectPHI units, GB stored) against the calibrated model. A gap over 20% points to a missing path or formula.

    Measurement collects aggregates only: counts, sums and percentiles, never message, note or transcript text. Groups smaller than 5 users are suppressed.
